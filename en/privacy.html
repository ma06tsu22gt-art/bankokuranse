BANKOKU RANSE Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 / Last updated: November 20, 2026

STEAM BASE ("we") explains below how the game "BANKOKU RANSE: Continent of War Maidens" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App has no account of our own. We do not collect your name or phone number, and no information is sent to our servers.

Your game progress (save data) and settings are stored on your device.

The first time you make an in-app purchase, we ask for your age group (under 16, 16–19, or 20 and over). This age group and the amount you have spent in the current month are stored only on your device, solely to apply the monthly spending limit for your age group, and are never sent anywhere.

Only when you use Data Transfer in Settings is your save data stored in Google Play Games services (Saved Games), linked to your Google account.

Reminders (such as stamina refills) are notifications scheduled on your device; we do not send them from a server. You can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message (including the app version in the subject line) only to reply to you and for no other purpose.

3. Advertising ID

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings at any time. If you purchase "No Ads" in the App, full-screen ads after battles are no longer shown (rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch them). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first and show personalized ads only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings. You can turn off ad personalization tied to your Google account at any time in Google's My Ad Center.

4. How we use information

We use only aggregated information from these services (such as ad impressions, revenue and stability) to operate and improve the App. The App does not use any analytics service that records how you play. We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. In-app purchases by users under 20 are subject to a monthly limit for each age group.

6. Security

Communication with these services is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Deletion (how to delete your data)

Data on your device: Uninstalling the App deletes all save data, settings and your age group.

Data saved through Data Transfer: You can delete it as this game's data from the settings of the Google Play Games app.

Advertising data: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. You may request deletion of information held by Google under Google's policies.

Emails you sent us: To request deletion, email the address below with "Data deletion" in the message. We will delete the information we hold (your emails) within 30 days and let you know. We hold no other information about you.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
